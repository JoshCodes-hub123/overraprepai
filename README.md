# OverraPrep AI

OverraPrep AI – FUTA**
*An AI-Powered CBT Exam Preparation and Tutor-Driven Quiz Platform*

---

## **1. Introduction**

The adoption of Computer-Based Testing (CBT) in universities such as the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA) has transformed student assessment. However, many students still face challenges such as inadequate practice resources, lack of proper guidance, poor feedback systems, and exam anxiety.

OverraPrep AI – FUTA is designed as an intelligent, AI-powered quiz and exam preparation platform that allows students to practice past questions and probable questions, receive AI explanations, and gain confidence before CBT exams. In addition, the platform introduces a **tutor marketplace model**, where verified tutors can upload premium quiz content and earn income based on student engagement.

---

## **2. Problem Statement**

The major challenges faced by FUTA students include:

* Limited access to well-organized and up-to-date past questions.
* Lack of personalized feedback during exam preparation.
* High exam anxiety due to unfamiliar CBT environments.
* Absence of a platform that fairly connects tutors with students in a structured, transparent manner.

There is a need for a centralized, intelligent system that combines **practice, understanding, mentorship, and monetization**.

---

## **3. Aim and Objectives**

### **Aim**

To develop an AI-powered CBT exam preparation platform that improves student confidence, understanding, and performance, while also enabling tutors to contribute content and earn revenue.

### **Objectives**

* To provide structured CBT practice using past and probable questions.
* To integrate AI-based explanations and tutoring.
* To allow tutors to upload quiz content after admin approval.
* To implement a token-based payment and revenue-sharing system.
* To track performance and learning progress of students.
* To create a sustainable academic ecosystem for students and tutors.

---

The project covers:

## **6. System Features**

---

### **6.1 Student Features**

* Account registration and login
* Access to free and premium quizzes
* Practice mode (untimed learning)
* CBT simulation mode (timed exams)
* AI explanations for questions and topics
* Performance analytics (accuracy, speed, weak areas)
* Token wallet system

### **6.2 Tutor Features (NEW – CORE INNOVATION)**

* Tutor application system
* Profile creation (qualification, courses, experience)
* Admin approval workflow
* Ability to create:

  * Courses
  * Topics

* View:

  * Number of students who attempted quizzes
  * Earnings dashboard

* Withdrawal request system

---

### **6.3 Admin Features**

* Approve or reject tutor applications
* Manage students and tutors
* Create and manage courses and topics
* Moderate uploaded quiz content
* Set platform commission percentage
* View platform-wide analytics
* Monitor payments and revenue distribution

---

## **7. Token & Revenue Model**

* Students purchase tokens within the app
* Tokens are used to unlock premium tutor quizzes
* When a student pays for a quiz:

  * A percentage goes to the tutor
  * A percentage goes to the platform
* Earnings are calculated automatically based on:

  * Number of students
  * Quiz price
  * Platform commission

This model ensures **fair monetization and sustainability**.

---

## **8. Artificial Intelligence Integration**

AI is used to:

* Explain quiz questions step-by-step
* Summarize topics clearly
* Recommend quizzes based on weaknesses
* Assist tutors in improving question quality
* Detect low-quality or duplicate questions
